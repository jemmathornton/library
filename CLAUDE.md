## How she likes it done (moved here from the Sims project’s notes, 19 Nov: the library keeps its own)

- **Covers, same printing:** a whole series from one printing (same publisher edition and year) matching her copies; lean to UK printings (she’s in the UK; Open Library pictures are often US). Flat, uncropped scans. Show each series as one row in reading order (her copies plus the proposal) and **ask before every cover change**, even her own ISBN’s picture. Libib ISBNs can be wrong: believe her description over the ISBN, and offer her own photos as the sure fix. UK sources: Oxfam’s shop API `https://onlineshop.oxfam.org.uk/ccstore/v1/products/HD_<n>` (ox_isbn13, ox_year, primaryFullImageURL + `&height=940`; one shop’s listings sit on neighbouring numbers) and World of Books `https://image-server.worldofbooks.com/images/<isbn13>.jpg`.
- **No generated text:** text on the site comes only from her words or the book’s own blurb, word for word; cuts only, never rewording. Missing means blank.
- **General fixes, not per item:** a problem on one book means fixing the rule for every book; say so in one line when reporting.
- **Show, don’t ask, for looks:** mock each option in the app, screenshot at 1280px, send them labelled, then ask. Words are fine for facts.
- **Suggestions are labelled** “my suggestion (not decided)”, and say who decided what.
- **TODO.md holds only open items;** a decided non-issue comes off.
- **Restarting the server:** only `explorer.exe "$env:APPDATA\Microsoft\Windows\Start Menu\Programs\Startup\Jemma's Library server.lnk"`, then check its parent is explorer.exe (started from Claude’s shell it dies with the session, 31 Oct).
- **When she’s away for hours,** keep working through a long backlog in one turn (checks, research, small fixes needing no decision); a final reply stops everything. Never write as if time has passed.

## Where things are

- `site/`: the app (`index.html`, `site.css`, `js/app.js`, `js/icons.js`, `js/data.js` **generated, never edit**). The only folder to upload if it goes online.
- `data/`: the source of truth. `shelves.json` (order, names, colours, which Libib collection), `books.json` (one entry per book; `id` is the Libib id; `displayTitle` overrides the shown title), `to-buy.json` (`maybeOwned` = book ids that look like it), `covers.json` (ISBN, or `id-<id>` for a book found by title, → cover link). `added.json`: books she added on the site (Add a book page; kept apart because a Libib re-import overwrites `books.json`). `bought.json`: to-buy books she has added to a shelf (“Got it”) or already owns; the build leaves them off the to-buy list. `no-cover.json`: Old Book Collection books that stay title cards rather than show a modern reprint’s cover (her rule, 18 Oct: old books never get modern covers). `removed.json`: Libib books she says aren’t real copies or is getting rid of; the build leaves them out. `shelf-moves.json`: shelves she chose for books Libib has on no genre shelf (18 Oct; the build applies them). Books she owns that were never in Libib are in `added.json` (with `why`), linked to her reading in `storygraph-links.json`. `details.json`: description, pages and publisher from Open Library (`tools/fill_details.py`), used only where Libib had none. `name-fixes.json`: Libib author names tidied (applied by the build). `series.json`: series in reading order (only ones whose order is sure; titles can have other spellings after `|`). Shown in place: on the shelves an author’s series sits together in reading order, and a book page says “Book n of …”; no series page (her choice, 18 Oct).
- `imports/libib-<date>/`: her Libib CSV exports as copied in (one per collection). `imports/storygraph-<date>/`: her StoryGraph export.
- `data/storygraph.json`: her reading, from StoryGraph (where she tracks it): status, dates, stars, moods, reviews. `books` = the shelf books (every copy) it matches; entries without `books` are books she doesn’t own (library books, ebooks). Read, reading or paused ones **sit on a genre shelf, cover faded with a walnut “Not owned” band** (her choice, 18 Oct, replacing the old Read elsewhere page): `data/elsewhere-shelves.json` maps StoryGraph title → shelf id (my guesses, for her to check; a new one needs a line there). They aren’t counted in a shelf’s own book count; Owned / Not owned filters on every shelf. Books she only means to read stay off the shelves.
- `data/reading-fixes.json`: reading statuses she has corrected (title → status), applied by the build over the import (Hooked is DNF, not paused; 18 Oct).
- **Phone scanning (30 Oct, her yes):** Binary Eye on her Android phone forwards each scan to `tools/serve.py`'s second listener, port 8021, open only on a network Windows calls Private (her home Wi-Fi; `ScanDoor`, 21 Nov) (`/scan?key=…&isbn=…` only: a valid 978/979 ISBN with the key goes on `data/scanned.json`, not in git; it serves no pages and can't add books). The key lives outside the project in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Jemmas Library\scan-key.txt`; the address and key show only on the Add a book page's “Set up phone scanning” (laptop only, `/api/scan-setup`). The Add page checks `/api/scanned` every 4 seconds, fills itself in from a new scan, and a book leaves the list when added. Port 8020 stays this-computer-only.
- **New reading comes in by itself:** `tools/serve.py` checks her Downloads every 5 minutes for a newer StoryGraph export (a CSV starting with StoryGraph’s own column list), imports it and rebuilds (only reads Downloads). Last one in `data/storygraph-source.json`, log in `logs/reading-updates.log`.
- `tools/import_storygraph.py <export.csv>` (report; `--matches` lists every match; `--apply` copies the export into imports/ and writes `data/storygraph.json`).
- `tools/import_libib.py <folder>` (report; `--apply` writes `data/books.json` and `data/to-buy.json`: genre collections are the library, the old “Jemma’s Library” catch-all only adds books that aren’t on a shelf, “Books to Buy” is the wishlist).
