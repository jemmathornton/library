## 4. The Shetland Witch (Kate Macdonald)

- **Current:** The Shetland Witch (Or, Atropos Wants Her Shears Back), then Stories from The Shetland Witch: In Achaea & Mrs Sinclair and the Feather Haa.
- **Confirmed:** the same. The novel came first: it was serialised on Substack from autumn 2023 and published in September 2024. The two novellas were serialised to paying subscribers in early 2024 and came out as a book in Feb 2025. The publisher (her own imprint) calls the book "two novellas, which spin off and expand aspects of the mother novel". Its blurb opens "In The Shetland Witch, …", so it assumes you have read the novel. Both novellas tell backstory: how Atropos came to Shetland, and the early 1800s. Even so, nothing presents them as a prequel to read first. The series has no numbers anywhere. Her new novel *Radical Magic* (1 Sep 2026) is a different world, not part of this series.
- **Sources:**
  - Peachfield Press (her imprint), book page: https://peachfieldpress.uk/stories-from-the-shetland-witch/
  - Barnes & Noble (a companion to The Shetland Witch): https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/stories-from-the-shetland-witch-kate-macdonald/1147268473
  - Kate Macdonald’s guest post on Fantasy Hive about serialising both. I only read it through search results, because the page refused the fetch: https://fantasy-hive.co.uk/2024/09/writing-and-publishing-the-shetland-witch-guest-post-by-kate-macdonald-the-shetland-witch/
- **Confidence:** high that the novel goes first. There is no official numbering, but none is needed.
- **Change:** none.

## 5. Solitaire (Alice Oseman)

- **Current:** Solitaire, Nick and Charlie, This Winter (publication order).
- **Publication order (confirmed):** Solitaire (31 Jul 2014), Nick and Charlie (16 Jul 2015), This Winter (5 Nov 2015). Her own site calls Nick and Charlie "the first of two Solitaire spin-off novellas" and This Winter the second.
- **What the author says:** her reading-order page says "My books can be read in any order." It also says both novellas can be enjoyed on their own. She gives no reading list, but the two timeline pictures on that page place each book:
  - **This Winter**: Christmas Day, year one. That falls during Heartstopper Volume 4 and just before Solitaire.
  - **Solitaire**: January–February, year two.
  - **Nick and Charlie**: June–July, year three.
  - So the story order is **This Winter, Solitaire, Nick and Charlie**. Goodreads numbers them the same way: This Winter is Solitaire #0.5, Nick and Charlie is #1.5.
- **Sources:**
  - Alice Oseman, "In What Order Should I Read Your Books?" (with both timelines): https://aliceoseman.com/ya-fiction/in-what-order-should-i-read-your-books/
  - The timeline pictures themselves: https://aliceoseman.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/table.png and https://aliceoseman.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Screenshot-2020-08-10-at-16_55_40.webp
  - Her book pages: https://aliceoseman.com/ya-fiction-book/nick-and-charlie/ and https://aliceoseman.com/ya-fiction-book/this-winter/
  - Goodreads: This Winter (#0.5) https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/27264289 · Nick and Charlie (#1.5) https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/25793458 · series https://www.goodreads.com/series/156843-solitaire
