# Library audit (23 Oct 2026)

Audit of HomeLibrary site/js/data.js (642 books, built 2026-10-18) against Open Library, 23 Oct 2026. Google Books could not be used: its anonymous daily quota was exhausted (HTTP 429). Open Library found 516 of 566 ISBNs. Every flag below was checked by hand; Open Library's own errors are listed separately so they aren't acted on.

## 1. ISBN vs title

No ISBN was confirmed wrong. Open Library's three mismatches were checked and are its own errors:

- **Army Without Banners** (9781912766789): Open Library says 'Living Stone' (Handheld Press); a library catalogue (TUM) confirms this ISBN is Army Without Banners, Handheld Classics 36.
- **The Book of Beginnings** (9780008612870): Open Library says 'Meeting Point'; booksellers confirm The Book of Beginnings, HarperCollins 2023.
- **DK Eyewitness Top 10 Delhi** (9781405370578): Same book (Top 10 Delhi); only the title wording differs.

Worth a look against the book:

- **Paper Towns** (9781101010938): 978-1-101 is a Penguin US ebook range and the date (2008-10-16) is the US ebook's; not found on Open Library. If she owns a paperback, the ISBN is probably wrong.
- **Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them: The Original Screenplay** (9781781109601): This is the Pottermore ebook ISBN (Libib's description says 'ebook edition'). A printed copy will have a Little, Brown/Sphere ISBN (UK hardback probably 9781408708989).
- **Becoming** (9780241983003): Added by hand; not found on Open Library or by web search. Check against the book.
- **The Magician's Apprentice** (9780316151337): Valid Little, Brown number but no record found anywhere; her other Canavans are UK Orbit (978-1-841...).
- **Others**: Not on Open Library but plausible (small presses, self-published, new): 46 others, listed in notFound in this file.

These seven are Pamela Cox's continuations (Open Library: Winter Term = Pamela Cox). Her Third Form and Sixth Form at St Clare's already list Cox + Blyton, so these are inconsistent.
